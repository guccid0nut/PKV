## 🏆 A Labor of Love: Standing on the Shoulders of Giants

Project Kongor exists for one simple, unshakeable reason: **we love Heroes of Newerth.**

This project is a humble, community-driven labor of love dedicated to keeping a classic game playable, accessible, and preserved—sharing the cleanroom platform services, documentation, and tools that we can so Newerth remains free for the community.

We owe an immense debt of gratitude to the visionaries, artists, engineers, and volunteers who created and cared for Newerth. This document is our public thank you.

---

## 🏛️ To S2 Games - The Visionaries & Engine Architects

To the original pioneers at **S2 Games**: **thank you.**

You built something truly special. In an era when many competitive games struggled with latency and input lag, you engineered some of the **crispest, most responsive, and most fluid netcode and movement mechanics in the MOBA genre**.

You created an unforgettable era of competitive esports for players across the globe.
