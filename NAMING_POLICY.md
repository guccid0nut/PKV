# Project KONGOR V (`PKV`) — Naming, Brand & Non-Affiliation Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy clarifies project identity, prevents confusion across the *Heroes of Newerth* preservation and modding ecosystem, and establishes clear boundaries between open-source software rights and project branding.

---

## 2. Independent Status & Non-Affiliation Disclaimers

1. **Not Affiliated with Publisher / Rights Holders:**
   *Heroes of Newerth*™, *Newerth*™, and all official retail characters, visual assets, audio, and trademarks are the property of **Garena Online Private Limited** (and historically **S2 Games** and **Frostburn Studios**). **Project KONGOR V (`PKV`)** (<https://github.com/guccid0nut/PKV>) is an independent, non-commercial software preservation and interoperability project and is **not** affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Garena, S2 Games, or Frostburn Studios. References to *Heroes of Newerth* are strictly nominative to describe protocol and file-format interoperability.
2. **Not Affiliated with Upstream `[K]ONGOR` or Kongor Studios (`HoN Reborn`):**
   **Project KONGOR V (`PKV`)** is an independent open-source fork and architectural continuation maintained by `guccidonut`. While PKV gratefully builds upon and credits the `AGPL-3.0` open-source foundations published by **`[K]ONGOR` (<https://github.com/Project-KONGOR-Open-Source>)**, PKV is operated independently and is **not** an official release of, nor endorsed by, `[K]ONGOR`, the original Project KONGOR staff, or **Kongor Studios (`HoN Reborn`)**.

## 3. Forking, Self-Hosting, TAO MCP Attribution & Community Master Servers

Under our open-source licenses ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE) — `AGPL-3.0-or-later`, and [`native/LICENSE`](native/LICENSE) — `MPL-2.0`), you are welcome to fork, modify, and self-host your own community Master Server, Chat Server, Web Portal, TAO MCP extensions, and custom lore timeline, subject to the following terms:

1. **Preserve Attribution & Origin Notices (`AGPL-3.0` §7(b)-(c)):** Keep [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), [`native/LICENSE`](native/LICENSE), [`NOTICE`](NOTICE), [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff), and [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) intact in source distributions, preserve `guccidonut` authorship notices on **TAO (Together As One) MCP** and PKV modules, and provide Corresponding Source links in modified network-facing portals as required by `AGPL-3.0` Section 13.
2. **Distinct Naming for Public Deployments & Forks (`AGPL-3.0` §7(c)/(e), `MPL-2.0` §2.3):**
   - Open-source code licenses do not grant trademark rights or permission to misrepresent modified forks or public server deployments as the official **Project KONGOR V (`PKV`)**, **TAO MCP**, or original **Project KONGOR** service.
   - If you operate a public server or publish modified builds, choose a distinct community name (for example, *"<Your Community Name> — Powered by Project KONGOR V"*) and clearly mark modifications so players and contributors know who operates the service.
3. **Non-Commercial Preservation Ethos:** Project KONGOR V accepts zero donations, sells nothing, and includes zero monetization hooks. See [`docs/charter.md`](docs/charter.md) and [`INTEROPERABILITY_AND_DMCA.md`](INTEROPERABILITY_AND_DMCA.md).
